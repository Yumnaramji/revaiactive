Three ways sports brands link lifestyle photos to the products in them, shown on your top row. Click the buttons and dots to try them.

A · Product names under the photo

Like Nike and Lululemon product rails: quiet, always visible, each name links to the product.

B · “Shop the look” button

Like Lululemon’s “Shop This Look”: one small button on the photo opens the list of pieces worn.

Click a button to open its list.

C · Hotspots on the garments

Shoppable-lookbook style: a “+” on each piece opens a mini card with name, price and a link.

Hover or tap a “+”.
